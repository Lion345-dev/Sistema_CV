## Sobre la empresa

S&P Dow Jones Indices (a division of S&P Global Inc., NYSE: SPGI) is the world’s largest resource for index-based concepts, data, and research. It produces iconic financial market indicators such as the S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average, alongside an extensive suite of equity indices across Latin America (e.g., S&P/BMV index family) and globally.

* **Industry:** Financial Information Services / Capital Markets Data & Benchmarks.
* **Scale:** S&P Global is a publicly traded, mega-cap global enterprise with over 35,000 employees worldwide. 
* **Data Note:** Specific operational headcount within the Index Management & Production Group (IMPG) in Mexico/LatAm is proprietary and should be treated as a focused business unit within S&P Global’s larger market intelligence infrastructure.

## Cultura y qué valoran

* **Rigor and Zero-Tolerance for Error:** Index maintenance directly dictates billions of dollars in benchmarked passive capital (ETFs, index funds). A miscalculated free-float or missed corporate action has immediate financial and regulatory consequences.
* **Autonomy and Ownership:** The position explicitly requires candidates who can "work independently" to own assigned coverage universes (countries/exchanges) without constant supervision.
* **Process Automation Mindset:** The group values candidates who actively eliminate manual data entry through Python, SQL, Power Automate, and modern AI/QA workflows rather than relying on spreadsheet manipulation.
* **Cross-Functional Precision:** Strong client focus combined with technical clarity—translating complex corporate restructuring events (spinoffs, tender offers) into clear actions for index committees and institutional clients.
