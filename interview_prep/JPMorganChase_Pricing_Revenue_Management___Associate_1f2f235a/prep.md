## Sobre la empresa

JPMorganChase is a global financial services firm and one of the largest banking institutions in the world. It operates across four primary business segments: Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management. 

The **LatAm Central Pricing Desk** in Mexico City operates primarily within the Corporate & Investment Bank / Commercial Banking framework (specifically Treasury Services and Payments). This division processes trillion-dollar daily transactional flows, competing directly with global peers such as Citigroup, HSBC, and Bank of America, as well as local regional institutions in Latin America.
